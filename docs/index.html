A few fair questions.

Is the model usage really free?

The kit uses Antigravity's free Individual allowance when your account and chosen model support it. Weekly limits apply. The adapter checks native credit overages before each consultation. It does not attest your subscription or invoice.

Does this replace Hermes' primary model?

No. Hermes calls the official AGY CLI for one selected question. Hosting and your Hermes primary model keep their own costs and configuration.

Is this a sandbox?

No. The role requests no tools. Observed tool or helper actions cause the adapter to reject the result after execution. These checks cannot undo actions. Use a clean workspace and sanitized context.

Is this an official Google or Nous Research project?

No. It is an independent MIT-licensed community kit using native interfaces. Upstream tools retain their own licenses and terms.

How can I support it?

Star the repository, share a useful workflow, or contribute a small improvement. No payment is required.
